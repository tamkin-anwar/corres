# TestFlight notes

## Build 19 (iPhone and iPad) and Mac build 20

Corres now speaks Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), German, Korean, French, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil). It follows your device's language, or you can pick one for Corres alone in Settings > Corres > Language.

On the Mac:
- Labels and folders in the sidebar. Drag conversations onto one to file them.
- Send Later, with a Send Later mailbox to change the time, send now or edit.
- Press Command-K to go anywhere or do anything. Press ? to see every shortcut.
- Notifications that arrive, with buttons that work.
- Needs You in the menu bar.
- A toolbar you can change (right-click it).
- Attachments open in Quick Look. Print with Command-P.
- Handoff with your iPhone, signatures, and you can make Corres your default mail app.
- Needs You and Waiting widgets for the desktop.

## Build 21 (iPhone and iPad) and Mac build 22

Faster
- Corres is much faster with a big mailbox. It opens on your newest mail right away, emails open almost at once, and syncing no longer slows everything down.
- The Mac app no longer freezes when it starts.
- The first time you open this build, Corres tidies how it stores your mail. It runs in the background for a minute or so and nothing changes on screen.

New
- Unread Only: tap the filter button above your mail to see just what's unread in Primary, Everything or any other tab. It replaces the Unread tab. On the Mac it's in the toolbar, or press Command-L.
- Search shows the best matches first, then everything else.
- Rules: sort mail as it arrives. Move it to a tab, keep it out of Needs You, mark it read or archive it.
- Templates for replies you write often.
- Mute a conversation so new replies stay quiet.
- Tap the sender at the top of an email to see all your email with them.
- Purchases and Attachments mailboxes, and searches you can save.
- Siri and Shortcuts can archive, reply to, forward and open your mail. There are Control Center buttons for New Email and Needs You, and you can mark something Handled right from the Needs You widget.
- When someone asks when you're free, Corres offers open times from your calendar.
- Works better with the largest text sizes and Increase Contrast. Suggested replies are written in the language of the email.

Fixed
- "Couldn't mark this conversation as read" no longer pops up.
- "Couldn't update this conversation" no longer appears after archiving or deleting.
- An email that won't load now says so and offers Try Again, instead of loading forever.
- Emails open at their full height instead of jumping as they load.
- Undo, Snooze and Handled now give a light tap you can feel.

Please try
- Opening Corres and how quickly your mail appears, on the iPhone and the Mac.
- Opening a few emails in a row.
- Unread Only on different tabs, and searching for a person or a company.

If anything feels slow or goes wrong, send it from Settings > Send Diagnostics.

## Build 23 (iPhone and iPad) and Mac build 24

New
- Corres now speaks Bengali (বাংলা). Pick it for Corres alone in Settings > Corres > Language.
- A new app icon, made for the glass look of iOS 26, with Clear, Tinted and Dark versions.
- Company logos beside companies' mail, from the logo each company publishes for email and checks with a certificate. Corres's server fetches them, so companies never see you. Turn them off in Settings > Private by Design.

Fixed
- The Mac app no longer freezes when it starts.
- Corres no longer quits when you leave it while iCloud mail is being checked.
- When Gmail asks apps to slow down, Corres waits and catches up instead of showing a sync error.
- Words fit everywhere: "All Inboxes" at the top, labels and empty screens no longer end in "…". Select is now a checkmark button.

## Build 25 (iPhone and iPad)

Corres 0.2.0 (25) for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

The big ones
- Corres is on Apple Watch. See what needs you, read the start of each email, and reply by voice, Scribble or a quick answer. Handled and Archive are there too. Not on your Watch yet? Open the Watch app on your iPhone and install it.
- Siri writes for you. Say "Write an email to Maya in Corres", change it by voice, then "Send it". Corres always asks before sending. Siri can search your mail in Corres too.
- Replies that sound like you. Suggested replies now use your own greeting, sign-off and length, learned on your iPhone from email you've sent. See what it learned in Settings > Composing.

Also new
- Remind Me: tap ⋯ on any email, then Add to Reminders, for this evening, tomorrow morning, when it's due, or no date. The reminder opens the email.
- On the Lock Screen: an email in Send Later counts down in its last hours, and a package out for delivery today stays until it arrives. Turn deliveries off in Settings > Notifications.
- Tap a sender's name to Archive All or Move All to Trash from them.
- iPad: press and hold a conversation, then Open in New Window. Drop files or photos onto an email to attach them.
- VoiceOver says what just happened, and Undo waits longer for you. The rotor jumps to Unread, Flagged and each message. Voice Control knows "Tap Delete" and "Tap Compose".

Fixed
- Previews no longer show "&zwnj;", "[cid:image…]" or rows of dashes.
- Corres opens faster.
- A rare crash when Corres stopped checking iCloud mail.

Please try
1. Siri: "Write an email to [someone] in Corres", then "Send it".
2. Reply to something from your Watch.
3. Add an email to Reminders, then tap the link in the reminder.

Something off? Settings > Send Diagnostics, or take a screenshot to send feedback through TestFlight.

## Mac build 26

Corres for Mac 0.2.0 (26)

The big ones
- Get New Mail is in the toolbar, and at the bottom of the sidebar next to "Updated". That spot also tells you when you're offline, or when an account can't sync, with Try Again.
- Your Mac reconnects and checks for mail the moment it wakes or gets back online.

Also new
- Remind Me: right-click an email, or Message > Add to Reminders.
- Click a sender's name to Archive All or Move All to Trash from them.
- Siri and Shortcuts can write and send email in Corres.
- Help > Send Diagnostics shares what Corres recorded if something goes wrong. Never your mail.

Fixed
- Previews no longer show "&zwnj;", "[cid:image…]" or rows of dashes.
- A crash when Corres stopped checking iCloud mail.
- Corres opens faster.

Please try
1. Get New Mail from the toolbar or the sidebar.
2. Leave your Mac asleep for a while, then wake it: new mail should arrive within seconds.

Something off? Help > Send Diagnostics, or send feedback through TestFlight.
