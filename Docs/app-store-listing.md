## App Review notes

> Corres is an email app. It works without signing in: on first launch, tap "Explore Corres" to see every screen with sample mail.
>
> To test with a real account, add an Outlook.com, Microsoft 365, iCloud or other email account in Settings → Add Account. Gmail sign-in works for Google test users only until Google finishes verifying the app; if you need a Gmail account, contact support@corres.app and we'll add one.
>
> Apple Watch: install Corres from the Watch app on the paired iPhone. The Watch shows what the iPhone sends it, so after "Explore Corres" it shows the same sample mail. Replies, Handled and Archive on the Watch are carried out by the iPhone.
>
> Corres Pro (Needs You, Waiting, answers in Search, summaries and suggested replies) is an auto-renewable subscription with a free trial, plus a Lifetime purchase; use a Sandbox account to test.
>
> Calendar access is optional and only asked for when you tap Check Your Calendar on an invitation; it is read on the device to show whether you're free, and never leaves it.
>
> All mail is stored on the device. A push relay receives only "mailbox changed" signals from Google and Microsoft to wake the app; it never receives message content. For iCloud, instant notifications are opt-in per account: the app-specific password is sealed on the iPhone so only our watcher service can open it, and the watcher only notices that new mail arrived, never reading a message. Summaries and suggested replies use Apple's on-device Foundation Models; Needs You, its reasons and key details also work on devices without Apple Intelligence.
